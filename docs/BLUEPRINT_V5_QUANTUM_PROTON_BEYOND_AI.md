## QUANTUM × PROTON × PHOTON × MATERIAL FORGE × ARSY
**Status: ARCHITECTURE PROPOSAL, not a deployed breakthrough or a revision of theological truth.**
**Goal:** human-operated, source-grounded, physics-aware research and mission ecosystem.

### 1. Names and core identities
- **EMPIRE OF THE LIGHT:** project/ecosystem umbrella.
- **A-L-L-A-H:** Accountable Life, Learning & Action Hub, product name, *not* divine authority.
- **100CENTER:** human operations and approval UI.
- **ARSY — Agent Reality System Yard & Orchestration:** proposed agency layer for agents, tools, execution contexts and governed workflows. AI models are a basic capability within ARSY, not its identity or full scope. The name does not claim the theological al-'Arsh.
- **HELIOGRID:** physical energy measurement/optimization and energy-system research; NOT an invented generator.
- **UNILEX:** multilingual, source-aware term definitions.
- **CORPUS:** primary source witnesses and comparative literature (with attributed traditions and versions).
- **SIDRA TREE:** proposed evidence/ontology graph.
- **MUNTAHA:** proposed epistemic, safety and authorization limit enforcement.
- **PHONNY:** *unratified* communication/mobile interface concept.
- **ZIIL:** protection, privacy, safety and accountability.
- **G.O.D.99 / WINGS10:** fictional organizational/duty registry, not supernatural grants.

### 2. Seven coherent system planes
**P1 HUMAN & OPERATIONS:** people, tenancy, consent, mission, Journey SELF/COMMUNITY/GLOBAL, review and 100CENTER.
**P2 AGENCY & ORCHESTRATION:** ARSY composes role-bounded agents, tools, retrieval, execution contexts and workflows; it coordinates task routing, handoffs, evidence, evaluation and human approval. Local or external AI models are one capability used by agents, alongside deterministic tools, simulations and other compute. Actions remain authorized, bounded and reversible.
**P3 ADVANCED COMPUTE:** classical CPU/GPU; optional HPC/QPU; photonic or neuromorphic lab adapters; independent benchmark before selection.
**P4 PHYSICAL ENERGY:** HELIOGRID meters grid/solar/battery/thermal availability; optional supervised PEM/hydrogen program; no free-energy claims.
**P5 MATERIAL DISCOVERY:** MATERIAL FORGE hypothesis → computational materials science → specimen → instrument data → repeatability → peer review.
**P6 KNOWLEDGE / COSMOLOGY:** UNILEX, original 4 scripture text families, separate Daoist/Shinto comparative libraries, physical cosmology, user testimony and interpretation.
**P7 GOVERNANCE & LIMITS:** human approvals, PRIME CODE, AMANAH/SHURA/MIZAN/AUDIT, ZIIL, MUNTAHA uncertainty boundary, cybersecurity and continuity.

### 4. Beyond AI does not mean beyond physics
"Beyond AI" = **beyond a single language model**, combining AI, simulation, formal verification, classical benchmarks, optional specialized processors, calibrated observations and independent human review. "Beyond quantum" = **heterogeneous compute**, not a claim to surpass quantum theory.
No consciousness proof, soul detector, prophecy certification, divine ranking, infinite-energy device, teleportation, or faster-than-light signaling is assumed.

### 5. Keep v4 engineering stack
**Monorepo:** pnpm + Turbo. **Admin:** Vite + React/TypeScript. **Public:** Next.js. **Backend:** Fastify as the canonical local Empire API; any public integration or production cutover still follows the external Gen2 ownership ADR. **Database:** local PostgreSQL via Empire Batteries Knex/Objection with transactional, additive model synchronization at API boot; `packages/db` Drizzle definitions are a prototype, not the runtime. **Module kit:** first-party allowlisted manifests, DAG dependencies, activation and audit. **Existing seven base modules:** kernel/research/scripture/journey/mission/governance/ziil remain, not replaced.
V5 adds **research capability packs**, not seven new public APIs: heliogrid, protonics, photonics, quantum, materials, energy-twin, multisource-comparative, hypercompute. No pack auto-enabled.

### 6. Epistemic contracts
Each claim is marked as one of SCRIPTURE_TEXT / TRADITION_INTERPRETATION / HISTORICAL_CLAIM / VERIFIED_MEASUREMENT / SCIENTIFIC_MODEL / HYPOTHESIS / PROJECT_NARRATIVE / PERSONAL_TESTIMONY. Sources, edition, translator, context, calibration, probability and licenses must be preserved. Faith claims do not become scientific measurements merely by similarity of words.

### 7. Safety / human leadership
Physical energy system requires qualified electrical engineers, independent protection, licensed hydrogen hardware; laboratory chemical, laser and compressed-gas risks get specific permissions. Privacy, minors, informed consent, retention and digital security are non-negotiable. G.O.D.99 and WINGS10 never grant operational authority. No arbitrary model may switch physical hardware without certified interlock and authorized action.

### 8. Delivery principle
First build one true operational ResearchRun → Job → Evidence → Approval in 100CENTER, then energy monitoring pilot, then simulation/advanced compute benchmarks, then authorized physical labs. Do not wait for quantum hardware before achieving useful service.
