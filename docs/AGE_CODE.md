# AGE CODE v1.1 — LIFE, CHARACTER & ROLE STANDARD

**Empire of the Light / A-L-L-A-H Command Center / ARSY / ZIIL**

**Status:** proposed baseline; not a live access-control policy, not a legal compliance certification.

## The three questions
1. **How old is the character?** Fictional *apparent* age and narrative setting; it is not an actual human age.
2. **How old must the human operator be?** **18+ baseline** for independently authorized operational roles. Additional licenses, qualifications, competence, child-safety training and local laws may impose further restrictions. Age never automatically makes a person eligible.
3. **How old are religious angels?** **UNKNOWN / NOT SPECIFIED AS A YEAR COUNT.** The Quran mentions angels in passages such as 35:1 and 66:6 but does not give a universal numeric age. WINGS 10 are **human departments and fictional depictions**, not real angels.

## AGE 5D ontology
- `AGE-HUMAN` = chronological age of a real person, often private.
- `AGE-VISUAL` = apparent age range of a fictional depiction.
- `AGE-TENURE` = duration of real service/mandate.
- `AGE-THEOLOGY` = age claims about religious entities, always attributed and not fabricated.
- `AGE-SYSTEM` = software time since initialization/release.

Never infer any dimension from the other one. A GOD-032 fictional character appearing 27 does not mean the person assigned GOD-032 is 27. A person assigned a WING does not become an angel.

## Human age bands (for access design, NOT a spiritual rank)
- CHILD: 0–12; protected participant only
- TEEN: 13–17; protected and supervised participant, where lawful
- YOUNG_ADULT: 18–29
- ADULT: 30–44
- MID_LIFE: 45–59
- SENIOR: 60+

There is **no upper age cutoff** for participation or leadership. Training, availability, actual competence, and mandate determine authorization. Children under 18 are covered by special protections under Indonesia's child-protection law; any real deployment must verify currently applicable local child and data laws.

## Human operators
A normal role assignment requires identity, purpose, explicit mandate, competency review and revocable access. For safeguarding-sensitive roles, require additional reviews and background checks where legally appropriate.

Youth must never be given independent authority over other users' personal information, emergency approval, high-risk financial transactions or irreversible actions. For education/learning, provide age-appropriate opt-in participation, protection and guardian controls as law requires.

## 99 personas / 10 WINGS integration
Keep registry identities unchanged; map `GOD99` and `WINGS10` to this policy rather than mutating each fictional entry or hardcoding fictitious birthdays. Distinguish team/department `createdAt` from member `ageBand`, character `apparentAge`, and service `tenure`.

No automatic “level up” from reaching age 21, 40 or 60. An experienced 19-year-old with the right training may be more appropriate for a low-risk role than an untrained 40-year-old; eligibility still depends on legal and role safeguards.

## Scripture, unknown values, and UNILEX
Record `QURAN:35:1` and `QURAN:66:6` as references about angels, **not evidence of numeric ages**. Represent AngelAge as `null` with an uncertainty status. Never encode `Infinity`, `immortal`, `1000 years`, or an AI-generated age as established scripture.

Map AGE_CODE's five distinct senses to UNILEX as future **reviewed** terms, not unapproved synonyms.
