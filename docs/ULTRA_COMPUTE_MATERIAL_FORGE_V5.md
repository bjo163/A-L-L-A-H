# HYPERCOMPUTE + Q-REALM + PHOTON/PROTON LAB + MATERIAL FORGE
**Status: PRE-IMPLEMENTATION RESEARCH. Not evidence of real quantum advantage, completed material invention or installed physical devices.**

## 1. ARSY COMPUTE ORCHESTRATION — a resource router, not a magical processor
Compute substrates: conventional CPU for reliable API/SQL/math; GPU for approved local inference and simulations; optionally HPC for batch science; photonic and protonic chips *only in supervised lab/partner research*; optional cloud quantum computer as experimental coprocessor. A resource router compares actual cost, time, accuracy, throughput and whole-system power.

\`\`\`text
Mission → validated claim & units → policy/authorization
  → reproducible classical baseline (CPU/GPU)
  → choose specialized method only when supported by reproducible benchmark
  → simulation / experiment → evidence/provenance
  → human scientific review → published finding or rejection
\`\`\`

**"Beyond AI"** here means using AI together with physics solvers, experimental measurements, human reasoning and verification. It does not assert machine consciousness or supersede physics.

## 2. Q-REALM (quantum research)
- Q01: classical quantum circuit simulation, software-only and reproducible.
- Q02: real cloud QPU circuit experiments by budget-approved providers with shot counts, noise/errors and reproducibility logs.
- Q03: quantum chemistry benchmark on small systems; compare classical accuracy and compute budget.
- Q04: external quantum sensing research, device calibration and controlled import.
- Q05: **post-quantum cryptography** migration plan using NIST FIPS 203 ML-KEM and FIPS 204/205 signatures; this is usable WITHOUT a QPU.
- Q06: fault-tolerant quantum future watch; target schedules from vendors are aspirational, not currently owned hardware.
- Quantum hardware is for specific classes of problem, not universal speedup. No faster-than-light communication through entanglement.

Required benchmark fields: \`task, operator, backend, circuit hash, qubit count, gate count, shots, fidelity/noise, precision, input provenance, classical baseline, runtime, cost, energy estimate, result CI, replicates, review\`.

## 3. PHOTON LAB
Research streams: photovoltaic absorption and optoelectronic materials; fiber/silicon photonics for communications; photonic AI accelerator lab studies; optically calibrated sensing. Evaluate end-to-end power, thermal cost, precision and fabrication/yield. Sources: Nature 2025 photon AI hardware and 2026 photonic neuromorphic prototypes. These are not plug-and-play commodity parts.

## 4. PROTON LAB
Separate two research themes:
A. Electrochemical proton transfer, PEM electrolyzers/fuel cells, membrane catalysts and longevity.
B. Protonic neuromorphic material circuits (e.g. protonic nickelate) for spatiotemporal memory/computation, measured in specialized facilities.
Never infer the second is a source of free electricity. Record ionic conductivity S/cm, humidity, temperature, switching lifetime, retention, sample formulation and equipment calibration.

## 5. MATERIAL FORGE — materials discovery protocol
Research candidate classes, NOT actual discovered novel material products:

| Candidate class | Potential application | Must measure / validate |
|---|---|---|
| Perovskite–silicon tandem | Advanced photovoltaics | PCE, lifetime, climate stress, lead containment, manufacturability |
| LFP / sodium-ion / solid state | Energy storage | Wh/kg, power, cycles, safety, operating temperature, recycling |
| Polymer PEM / protonic ceramics | Electrolytes and fuel cells | Proton conductivity, selectivity, durability, catalyst loss |
| Protonic nickelates | Neuromorphic circuits | Switching thresholds, endurance, memory retention, error |
| MOFs & porous adsorbents | H2 storage, water/CO2 capture | Capacity at real conditions, regeneration energy, cycling |
| Graphene & 2D layers | Sensors, electronics, interfaces | Yield, long-term reliability, contact resistance, safety |
| Optical waveguide/metasurface | Integrated photonics | Optical loss, process compatibility, accuracy, thermal load |
| Catalysts | Electrolysis and PEM fuel cells | overpotential, degradation, precious-metal usage, cost |
| Thermal phase-change media | Passive temperature regulation | flammability, cycles, thermal conductivity, material stability |

Pipeline: licensed literature/datasets → chemical formula & crystal structure → quality gate → computational modeling (DFT/classical baseline) → physics-constrained AI hypothesis → optional quantum calculation → authorized lab sample → XRD/SEM/Raman/electrochem tests where relevant → independent replication → expert review → open release or IP/legal hold.
A simulated prediction is **not** a synthesized or certified material.

## 7. Safety
Compressed hydrogen, high voltage, lasers, cryogens, radiological/proton beams and toxic chemical processes have specialist safety requirements. No unlicensed DIY construction or online commands that actuate hazardous equipment. For all real experiments, use accredited/qualified facilities, regulatory approvals and documented hazard assessment.
