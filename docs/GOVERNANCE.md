# HUMAN GOVERNANCE, ACCESS AND SAFETY

## Actor != Persona != Department != AI Agent
Actor: real responsible user, organization or service identity.
Persona: fictional/duty alias; **never** authorizes API permissions.
Department: organizational unit with scoped mandates.
AI agent: tool-executing software principal with narrower permissions than its authorized human operator.

Mandates: subject, purpose, authority scope, approver, expiration, revocation, permitted tools and audit reference. No approval by silence. Privacy and tenant isolation by default.

## Responsibility flow
- RĀ’ID proposes Path and documents alternatives.
- WITNESS/GUIDE verifies evidence and communicates uncertainty.
- ZIIL reviews risks, consent, child protection where relevant and harm handling.
- SHURA records consultation, dissent and appeal route.
- An authorized HUMAN approves activation.
- MUJADDID/RENEWAL STEWARD owns maintenance, review cadence, rollback and retirement.

## No artificial religious authority
System cannot declare a human angel, prophet or god, grant divine powers, issue religious judgments as facts, or score salvation/sin. Religious references are attributed research records; fictional characters are narrative/operational abstractions.

## Minimum controls
OIDC/authentication integration (later); least-privilege authorization; clear data retention policies; consent withdrawal; PII minimization; human-visible audit logs; bounded agent tool access; secret separation; queue retry/idempotency; incident response; backups/restore proof; copyright/source provenance.
