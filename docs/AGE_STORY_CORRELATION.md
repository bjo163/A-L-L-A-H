## Why revise the earlier numbers?
G.O.D. 99 had an arbitrary **18–39** fictional visual style and WINGS 10 an arbitrary **25–45** human-representative appearance. The user established a more specific narrative about **19, under 40, age 40, 40 days/nights, 63 lunar years, 100+ years, 950, 969, 15 added years**, and ageless/unknown categories.

Those are not one age scale. They must retain **separate dimensions and source classes**. In particular, the under-40 *WE ARE SAVAGES* story motif does **not** make a moral or faith-based membership eligibility test.

## Hero / WINGS policy: correlation without conflation
- **G.O.D. 99**: fictional adult visual identity 18–39 can **echo an under-40 story motif**; this is an aesthetic narrative choice only. Real operators may be any qualified adult age **18+**, with **no upper age restriction**.
- **WINGS 10**: the organization/departments **have founding dates, not birthdays**. Human depictions 25–45 are **visual suggestions, not ages of Jibril, Mikail, Israfil or any real angels**.
- **19-year-old case**: review capacity, dependency, responsibility, support and individual circumstances; age never determines moral salvation/condemnation or permission automatically.
- **40 years**: add a **Life Milestone** marker to Journey records as OPTIONAL reflection; do not change the legal adult age baseline (18) or grant automatic appointment.
- **100 years or more**: may be represented as `aspirationalLifeGoal` **only upon user consent**, never as a guarantee of lifespan or a system-driven supernatural effect.
- **40D40N**: store as `MISSION_DURATION`, with start/end dates and timezone; any Subuh/moon/astronomy record is an ordinary observation, not evidence of age modification.
- **63 lunar vs solar**: require `calendarSystem`, `startDate`, `endDate`, `calculationMethod`, `source`, `uncertainty`. No silent conversion, age invention or mistaken narrative-history equivalence.

## Claims and source interpretation
- **Qur'an 46:15** ties reaching forty with reflection on gratitude and family; it does not say that being younger than forty is a unique universal faith class.
- **Qur'an 29:14** states a thousand minus fifty years **among Noah's people**, not the total length of his entire life.
- **Isaiah 65:20** speaks in a particular restoration-prophecy setting; don't claim present-day people are cursed for dying before one hundred.
- **Genesis 5:27** says Methuselah's life was 969 years within Genesis, which is a tradition-specific textual claim.
- **Isaiah 38:5 / 2 Kings 20:6** concern fifteen added years for Hezekiah, not a software capability.
- **Luke 3:23** says *about* thirty at the ministry's beginning.
- **Qur'an 23:99–100** includes a requested return after death and a barrier; AI cannot certify return-to-life, age reset, death reversal, immortality, or eschatological events.

For all four source families, preserve **source text vs tradition vs human narrative vs empirical assessment**; no forced equivalence.

## Engineering rules
1. All narrative age correlations have stable IDs, units, source class, interpretation and human-review status.
2. All citations are URLs of reference locations; a URL does **not** mean a copyrighted full text has been ingested.
3. Story numbers are **never** converted into hard user permissions, age-based promotions, faith ranks, medical predictions, legal rulings or divine assertions.
4. Actual human DOB is private and optional; use verified adult flag or age band wherever feasible.
5. Code tests reject absent IDs, undocumented year units, missing sources for scripture claims, invented numerical angel ages and unauthorized narrative-based authorization.
