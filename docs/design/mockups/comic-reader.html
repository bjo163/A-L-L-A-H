Responsive + interaction notes
Desktop: contents, single-page canvas, and controls share one reader row. Tablet: the one-image page spans the row and supporting panels sit below. Mobile: navigation wraps beneath the artwork, then contents and explanatory notes follow. Button state, page count, headings, alt text, visible focus, and arrow-key navigation make the prototype inspectable.
Rights / implementation handoff
Both panels are original geometric SVG samples embedded here, with no borrowed imagery and no claim of production clearance. The real reader needs approved page assets, creator/original credits, source classification, public revision and edit dates from the editorial API. Those data are placeholders in this design exercise; no backend exists in this mock.
