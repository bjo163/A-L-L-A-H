Design acceptance
At 320–390 px, navigation collapses and constellation labels remain legible without sideways scrolling. At 768 px, supporting panels stack beneath the network. At 1440 px, the page uses a two-column layout. Keyboard users can reach every link and button; focus is high contrast. Reduced-motion disables the decorative spinner animation; forced-colors preserves borders and labels. Sample role connections are not assertions about actual staff.
