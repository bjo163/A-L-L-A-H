# Contributor privacy, consent, and media rights

**Status: editorial checklist; not legal advice.** The repository now has
tenant-scoped Moonwitness editorial models and a publication workflow, but this
checklist is not itself evidence that a person consented, that rights were
granted, or that a legal review occurred. The editorial workflow does not yet
persist the complete contributor/media-rights evidence described below.

## Before accepting testimony or identifiable material

- [ ] Record the contributor's identity/contact details only in a restricted
  system, separately from public copy. Do not place private details in source,
  issue comments, fixtures, or public metadata.
- [ ] Record informed, specific, affirmative consent for the intended material,
  audience, channels, attribution choice, and publication period. Consent must
  be freely given and revocable; silence, submission, or a fictional persona is
  not consent. Record who obtained it, when, and where the evidence is held.
- [ ] Record privacy review and the redacted public derivative. Remove or
  generalize sensitive locations, contact details, identifying combinations,
  and details that could expose another person. Keep the restricted source
  separate and access-limited; do not rely on blurring alone for metadata.
- [ ] For minors or uncertain age, stop publication pending safeguarding and
  jurisdiction-appropriate guardian/participant consent and a named qualified
  human review. Do not collect extra identity or age data merely to fill this
  checklist.

## Before publishing media or quotations

For each image, poster, audio/music, video, lyric excerpt, quotation, or other
third-party asset, record: asset identifier; source URL or acquisition record;
creator/author when known; rights holder; exact license or written permission;
permitted channels, edits, attribution, territory, and term; restrictions;
reviewer and review date. A blank, unknown, expired, incompatible, or
non-commercial license is **not cleared**. Do not publish full copyrighted
lyrics or reproduced visuals unless a qualified rights reviewer has documented
an applicable permission or other approved basis. Do not treat public
availability, attribution, or AI generation as proof of rights.

## Retention, withdrawal, correction, and unpublishing

- Set a documented retention period and deletion owner before collection; retain
  the minimum source evidence needed to honor the consent and rights record.
- Provide a private route to withdraw consent, correct attribution, or request
  removal. A qualified human reviews identity/authority and scope without
  exposing the request publicly.
- On a substantiated withdrawal or rights change, pause distribution promptly
  while review occurs. Remove or replace affected public copies and media,
  invalidate caches/derivatives where controlled, and record the action and
  remaining copies outside the public record. Preserve a restricted audit
  reference only when justified by the documented retention policy.
- An immutable published revision does not override withdrawal. Mark it
  withdrawn/unavailable, prevent new access, and create a corrected revision
  only after re-review. Explain any copies that cannot be recalled (for example,
  third-party downloads) to the requester; do not promise retroactive erasure.

## Publication decision (fail closed)

The responsible editor must confirm all applicable items before release:

1. Consent is present, specific to this use, current, and not withdrawn; or the
   content contains no real contributor testimony/identifying material.
2. Privacy redaction and safeguarding review are complete; no restricted source
   data is included in the public derivative or its metadata.
3. Every included asset has a recorded, current, compatible rights basis and
   required attribution; otherwise replace/remove it.
4. Retention, removal contact, and withdrawal handling are assigned.
5. A named human reviewer records the decision, date, and exact revision/asset
   identifiers. No checklist completion or AI output substitutes for approval.

If any required item is missing or unknown, the decision is **blocked** and the
revision stays unpublished. Current publication guards enforce editorial
state, tenant boundaries, reviewer/publisher permissions, and immutable
publication snapshots. The separate Signal ledger records contributor and
testimony consent state and rejects sensitive-location disclosure. However,
there is not yet a persistent per-asset rights/attribution record or a publish
guard that requires rights clearance; the editorial publishing flow does not
yet consult Signal consent/revocation or this checklist. Unpublishing prevents
the current public projection from serving the story, but automated cache and
third-party copy invalidation are not guaranteed. Do not describe those rights
and withdrawal controls as runtime-enforced until implemented and verified.
