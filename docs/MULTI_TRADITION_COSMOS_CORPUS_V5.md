# UNIVERSAL COSMOS CORPUS V5 — FOUR PRIMARY SOURCES + COMPARATIVE WORLD TRADITIONS
**Aim:** multilingual comparative knowledge, not forced unification of all religions or an empirical detector of unseen entities.

## Source families stay distinct
The existing initial **four primary text families** remain:
- \`SRC-QUR\`: Qur'an, with Arabic mushaf/reading identity, translations and tafsir separate.
- \`SRC-TOR\`: Torah/Pentateuch textual witnesses and editions, while Islamic *Tawrat* remains a distinct theological referent.
- \`SRC-ZAB\`: Psalms/Mazmur textual witnesses and numbering schemes, while Islamic *Zabur* remains a distinct referent.
- \`SRC-INJ\`: Christian canonical Matthew/Mark/Luke/John text witnesses, while Islamic *Injil* remains a separate theological referent.
This does not demote other sacred texts or claim these four exhaust humanity's religious knowledge.

## New optional comparative literature — NOT "fifth equal revealed book"
| Concept family | Works/source clusters to register | Context boundary |
|---|---|---|
| \`CMP-DAO\` | Dao De Jing / Laozi (editions & translations), Zhuangzi, Daozang specific works | Dao/Tao and qi have tradition-specific conceptual histories; not measurable electrical power/photons/protons by lexical substitution |
| \`CMP-SHIN\` | Kojiki, Nihon Shoki, Engishiki norito, shrine sources and academic criticism | kami, ritual purification, land and nature relationships are not synonymized with Qur'anic angels or "ruh" |
| \`CMP-BUD\` | Buddhist Nikaya/Agama, Mahayana sutras, Tibetan collections: optional after expert scoping | Complex plural Buddhist traditions, never a single interchangeable cosmology |
| \`CMP-HIND\` | Vedas, Upanishads, Bhagavad Gita and specific schools: optional | Different textual histories and cosmological/ritual frameworks |
| \`CMP-ORAL\` | Oral / Indigenous cosmologies only with community consent and benefit agreements | Do not ingest restricted/sacred materials without permission |

**SHINTO NOT "THE RELIGION OF SOULS":** The term kami, ancestor/spirits and practices belong to many nuanced traditions. **DAO/QI NOT LAB POWER:** Qi has religious/philosophical contexts; watts and joules are measured physical quantities. **RUH NOT H+ PROTON:** scripture's ruh cannot be translated into an ionic conductivity value or a soul-detection sensor.
Universal studies allow comparisons and disagreement, never grade faith, rank traditions, declare supernatural identities, or rewrite an original source.

## Source/claim classes
\`SCRIPTURE_TEXT, RITUAL_LITURGY, TRADITION_INTERPRETATION, HISTORICAL_ATTESTATION, EMPIRICAL_MEASUREMENT, SCIENTIFIC_MODEL, PERSONAL_TESTIMONY, PROJECT_STORY, AI_HYPOTHESIS\`.
Every record: \`sourceFamilyId,workId,witnessId,editionId,canonScheme,language,passageRef,translator,rights,sourceURI,date,contentChecksum,reviewer,contextTags\`.
Comparative relation: \`relatedTo / distinctFrom / inspiredBy / broaderThan / narrowerThan\`; exactMatch requires editorial proof of same scope, never lexical similarity alone.
Store contradictions/disagreements as a feature, not a bug.

## Cosmos ontology with parallel lanes
- **COS-OBS:** scientifically observed astronomical time, sky objects, data and uncertainty.
- **COS-PHYS:** physical models of particles, fields, energy, spacetime and material properties, with empirical status.
- **COS-REL:** attributed religious cosmologies and sacred narrative; not mapped as instrument observations.
- **COS-PHIL:** philosophical and comparative models e.g. Dao, natural harmony, causality, conceptual ontology.
- **COS-HIST:** dated manuscripts, translations, schools and documented human history.
- **COS-USER:** project narrative/testimony explicitly attributed and private by default.
- **COS-SYM:** symbolic stories, game metaphors and creative aesthetics.
Cross-lane relations may be *analogy/hypothesis*, but never silently convert one kind into another.

## Qur'anic value inspirations without scientific falsification-by-faith
- Qur'an **3:190–191**: contemplation of heavens, earth, and night/day.
- Qur'an **49:6**: investigate reports before acting harmfully.
- Qur'an **4:58**: entrusted responsibilities and justice (governance interpretation).
These verses do not specify quantum gates, fuel-cell efficiency, or a new material formula.

## Daoist/Shinto source reliability
- Oxford Bibliographies: Daoism, Laozi, Zhuangzi; Daoist Canon historically plural.
- Kokugakuin University Encyclopedia of Shinto: Norito and relation to Engishiki, Kojiki and Nihon Shoki.
- Do not import copyright-protected translations or liturgies without license check.
- Community-facing religious comparison should retain community context, avoid devotional imposition, and allow corrections/appeals.

## Acceptance criteria
- Search returns exact source, edition, original term, translation provenance, kind of claim and missing-data warning.
- Query "Dao energy, ruh, proton" must show **distinct meanings** and not output a measurable "spirit field".
- Query "Injil" must distinguish Qur'anic religious referent from four canonical Christian Gospel textual collections.
- Shinto classifications must allow multiple contextual interpretations rather than one "spirit religion".
- Cross-tradition mapping must be human reviewed; no truth ranking.
