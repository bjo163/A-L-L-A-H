## 100CENTER UI screen mapping — no sidebar explosion
- **OVERVIEW**: system real health/readiness, approved mission status, energy usage only when sensors installed, alert evidence.
- **RESEARCH RUNS**: Corpus & UNILEX, Quantum Lab experiments, Proton/Photon papers, Materials simulation and source compare as context-specific tabs.
- **OPERATIONS**: authorized Jobs/Evidence, HELIOGRID metering, renewable energy costs, mission pipeline, ZIIL safety status.
- **SETTINGS**: operator identity, approved module manifest/catalog, integrations, device configurations, limits and audit accessibility.
- **PUBLIC NEXT PORTAL**: only published research/education/results with source and safety review; no secrets, children's data or private missions.

## Specific acceptance and anti-fiction gates
G-V5-0 — Research/ADR: owners and approved external standards; no simulated findings described as installed physical hardware.
G-V5-1 — Core Ops: deployed auth/API/db/mission/audit/restart and backup proof.
G-V5-2 — Physical metering: calibrated sensors, units, transfer efficiency, monitored outages and safety plan.
G-V5-3 — Materials: at least one fully reproducible scientific baseline & known candidate evaluation; no fabricated new element/material.
G-V5-4 — Quantum/photon/proton: independently reproducible comparison; noise/precision and total energy/cost measured.
G-V5-5 — Comparative corpus: license, edition, original term, context and disambiguation; no forced equivalence.
G-V5-6 — Security: OIDC, tenant RBAC, post-quantum migration assessment, encrypted secrets and signed instrumentation.
G-V5-7 — Production: proper qualified oversight, emergency procedures, data minimization, multi-site rollout and reversal.

## Physical site decisions BEFORE equipment purchases
Get actual site power bill, device watt-meter log, local solar irradiance (seasonal), weather, uptime target, trained installer quote, hardware warranty, electrical protection plan, permitting, qualified maintenance and emergency procedures. No generator sizing based on conceptual names alone.

## Proposed acceptance scenario
An operator requests a licensed, traceable study of improved solar PV material:
1. The requester consents and provides scope.
2. UNILEX parses concepts and source families.
3. MATERIAL FORGE retrieves papers/citations and checks versions/licenses.
4. ARSY dispatches *classical DFT or pre-approved simulation* with known baseline.
5. Optional quantum route may be benchmarked but must never be required.
6. Output includes units, uncertainty, compute cost, energy spent, methodology and reviewer.
7. A qualified researcher verifies; no untested hardware control is issued.
8. 100CENTER shows provenance, result status SIMULATED, next tests, approvals and audit.
9. Release to public portal only after publication review.

**Bottom line:** "ultra futuristic" is an R&D roadmap with measured experiments, not a license to skip thermodynamics or claim real supernatural technology.
