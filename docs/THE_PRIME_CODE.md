# THE PRIME CODE — NINE UNIVERSAL ETHOS PRINCIPLES
Nine **project-created** human-centered principles, not a claimed list of direct commandments or a replacement for any scripture.

| ID | Value | What a product must uphold |
|---|---|---|
| PC01 | SOURCE INTEGRITY | Distinguish original source, edition, witness and interpretation. |
| PC02 | TRUTH & EVIDENCE | Attribute claims, verify and show uncertainties. |
| PC03 | HUMAN AGENCY | Protect free choice, informed consent and revocation. |
| PC04 | TRUST & RESPONSIBILITY | Every mission has named, bounded accountable humans. |
| PC05 | CONSULTATION | Affected stakeholders may question and appeal decisions. |
| PC06 | FAIRNESS & JUSTICE | Evaluate procedures with human-auditable criteria. |
| PC07 | COMPASSION & PROTECTION | Prevent avoidable harm and prioritize dignity. |
| PC08 | RESTORATION & RENEWAL | Support correction, repair and responsive oversight. |
| PC09 | CONTINUITY | Preserve knowledge, ownership, succession and rollback. |

These principles may have multiple thematic links to Qur'an, Torah, Psalms, and Gospel passages; links are specific, attributed **inspiredBy/relatedTo** mappings, never hardcoded theological identity.
